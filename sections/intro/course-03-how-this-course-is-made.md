# How This Course Is Made

Authors map each section to a published KCNA objective and check technical explanations against primary documentation. They review examples for accuracy, diagrams for clear component responsibilities and practice questions for one supported answer. No command output is invented. The labs and Playgrounds are built from static checks of their configuration and are marked as untested until a maintainer runs them against a cluster.

Structural checks verify the manifest, the reading paths, the section introductions and the summary of every module. Technical review remains necessary before publication. An exam objective can change. Maintainers record the date used for the curriculum map and revise the material when needed.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS037/graphs/contributors) maintain this course. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS037/issues), include the page title, the incorrect statement and an explanation of the expected behavior. Suggest a focused change so that reviewers can check it against the objective.

The repository [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS037/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse. Exam objectives belong to their respective owners. The lessons and practice questions are independently written.
